Pink Diary Lock & Study Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Published by HamsterDragon Studios

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the Pink Diary Lock & Study Notes app (shown on your device as "Petal") for Android, available on Google Play and published by HamsterDragon Studios ("we", "us", "our"). HamsterDragon Studios is the developer name of Harrish Desikan Varatharajan, the individual developer and legal entity responsible for this app, based in Tamil Nadu, India.

Summary

Pink Diary Lock & Study Notes (Petal) is a notes and journaling app by HamsterDragon Studios. We do not require an account, we do not run our own servers, and we never receive your notes. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs. Your notes, photos, voice memos and files are stored on your device. A few optional features use Google services to work: place search, map links, address lookup for your current location, and Petal Pro purchases through Google Play. Each is explained below.

Information we collect

We (HamsterDragon Studios) do not collect, store or have access to your notes, attachments, location or any other personal data. Some optional features send limited data directly from your device to Google, which processes it to provide that feature: • Place search: the text you type into the place search box. • Address lookup: your device's approximate coordinates, when you tap to add your current location. • Petal Pro: your purchase and subscription status, handled by Google Play. Nothing is sent unless you use one of these features.

Where your content lives

Notes, folders, tags, moods, reminders, voice memos, photos, captured images and attached files are stored locally in Petal's private app storage on your device. Deleted notes go to Trash and are permanently removed after 30 days, or immediately when you empty Trash. All content is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall Petal.

Device backups

Petal allows Android's built-in backup. If you have turned on Google backup on your device, Android may include Petal's app data (including your notes) in your device backup stored in your Google account. This backup is created, encrypted and controlled by Android and Google, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn this off in your device Settings › Google › Backup. Exported files (JSON or Markdown) are saved or shared wherever you choose.

Camera permission

Why: so you can take a photo and attach it to a note from inside Petal. When: only when you tap the camera action. What happens to the photo: it is saved into Petal's private app storage on your device and attached to your note. It is not uploaded to us or analyzed. You can revoke this permission any time in Settings › Apps › Petal › Permissions. Petal keeps working; only in-app camera capture becomes unavailable.

Microphone permission

Why: so you can record a voice memo and attach it to a note. When: only while you are actively recording. There is no background listening. What happens to the audio: the recording is saved into Petal's private app storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us, transcribed or analyzed. You can revoke this permission any time in Settings › Apps › Petal › Permissions.

Photos & files access

When you attach an image or file, Android opens its system picker. Petal only receives the specific item you choose. Petal does not request broad gallery or storage permissions and cannot read your photos or files in the background.

Location

Why: so you can attach a place or your current location to a note. Current location: if you tap to add your current location, Petal asks for the location permission and reads your device's location once. Android's address lookup service (provided by Google) converts the coordinates into a readable address. The result is saved only inside that note on your device. Place search: when you search for a place, the text you type is sent to the Google Places API to show suggestions, and the place you pick is looked up to get its name and coordinates. Your device location is not sent with these searches. Opening a place: tapping a saved place opens it in Google Maps or your maps app. Petal never tracks your location in the background, and we never receive your location. You can deny or revoke the location permission at any time; you can still type a place manually.

Petal Pro subscription

Petal Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see or store your payment card or billing details. Petal receives only your purchase status from Google Play so it can unlock Pro features, and stores a Pro flag on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in the Google Play Store › Payments & subscriptions. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Reminders & notifications

Why: to send note reminders you set and an optional daily journaling reminder. Petal schedules these on your device using Android's alarm system and re-schedules them after your phone restarts. Reminder notifications may show the note's title. You can turn off the daily reminder in Petal's settings, or turn off notifications for Petal in your device settings.

App lock & biometrics

If you enable App Lock or lock individual notes, Petal asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. Petal never sees or stores your biometric data or device PIN; Android only tells Petal whether verification succeeded. Locked notes are hidden behind this check inside the app. They are stored in Petal's private app storage like other notes.

Contacts, calls & messages

Petal does not read your contacts, call log or messages. When you tap Share, WhatsApp, Messages or Call, Android hands the action to that other app and you choose what is sent. Petal never sends anything on your behalf.

Internet access

Petal uses the internet only for the Google features described above: place search, address lookup, map display, and Google Play purchases. Petal does not upload your notes, photos, voice memos or files anywhere. The rest of the app works fully offline.

Third-party services

Petal uses these Google services, each governed by the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy): • Google Places API and Google Maps, for place search and maps • Android Geocoder (provided by Google Play services), for address lookup • Google Play Billing, for Petal Pro purchases Petal contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not use the Google Advertising ID.

Sharing & exporting

When you copy, share, export, send to WhatsApp or Messages, or call from a note, your content is handed to the destination app or contact at your explicit request. Once it leaves Petal it is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Security

Your notes live in Petal's private, sandboxed app storage, which Android protects from access by other apps. App Lock adds a fingerprint, face or PIN check before your notes are shown. We recommend keeping a device screen lock enabled, since anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to open your content.

Children's privacy

Petal is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal data, no child-specific data is collected by us.

Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Because your content stays on your device and we do not receive it, there is nothing for us to access, correct, port, restrict or delete on our side. You control your data directly: edit or delete any note in the app, empty Trash, clear all data via Settings › Apps › Petal › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app. For data processed by Google (place searches, address lookups, purchases), you can use the controls in your Google account. We do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA / CPRA. If you have any privacy request, contact us using the details below.

International transfers

We do not transfer your data. When you use place search, address lookup, maps or purchases, Google may process that limited data on servers outside your country under its own privacy policy and safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the "Last updated" date above. For material changes we will also show a notice in the app.
